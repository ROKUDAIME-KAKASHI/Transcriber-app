## Phase Goal

**As a** person recording a meeting on my phone with the free on-device engines, **I want to** see who said what, computed on the device, **so that** my transcript, exports and AI minutes attribute statements to the right people without internet and without slowing live transcription.

<objective>
Give the existing speech worker a third, lowest-priority job class that turns each finished phrase into a speaker embedding (D-01, D-04, D-05), and expose it through a small client API that plan 07 (useRecorder) and plan 06 (offline readiness) consume.

Purpose: SPK-01 needs a vector per phrase computed on-device; SPK-05 needs those vectors to never delay transcription (trap 1: the audio buffer is transferred, so the embedding must ride on the same message).
Output: SPEAKER_MODEL in models.ts, pure embedSchedule.ts + tests, worker embedding class, client API.
</objective>
