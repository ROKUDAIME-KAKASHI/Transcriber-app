## Phase Goal

**As a** person recording a meeting on my phone with the free on-device engines, **I want to** see who said what, computed on the device, **so that** my transcript, exports and AI minutes attribute statements to the right people without internet and without slowing live transcription.

<objective>
Make speaker names flow everywhere text leaves the transcript: one shared `speakerLabel(n, names)` (D-10) used by TXT, Word, HTML/PDF exports and the AI minutes route, plus the D-12 prompt change and the per-chunk multiSpeaker bug fix (SPK-04).

Purpose: SPK-03 ("rename once, applies to exports and minutes") and SPK-04 (minutes attribute to speakers for on-device recordings). The UI rename (plan 05) only has to update `speakerNames`; everything here reads it.
Output: speakerLabel + tests, export builders taking names, process route reading speakerNames.
</objective>
