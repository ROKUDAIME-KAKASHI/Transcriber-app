## Phase Goal

**As a** person recording a meeting on my phone with the free on-device engines, **I want to** see who said what, computed on the device, **so that** my transcript, exports and AI minutes attribute statements to the right people without internet and without slowing live transcription.

<objective>
Extend the Phase 2 local-first layer for diarization: a Dexie v2 `embeddings` table (D-09), repo helpers, a `relabelPending` flag, and a sync speakers step that pushes refined labels through the existing `PATCH /api/meetings/:id/speakers` route (D-08, trap 3).

Purpose: SPK-02 requires refined labels to be "saved with the meeting", which means reaching the server, not only Dexie; the tail sync never resends old parts. SPK-03 names already ride the same step.
Output: types/db/repo changes, pure speakersRequest + tests, sync.ts speakers step.
</objective>
