## Phase Goal

**As a** person recording a meeting on my phone with the free on-device engines, **I want to** see who said what, computed on the device, **so that** my transcript, exports and AI minutes attribute statements to the right people without internet and without slowing live transcription.

<objective>
Wire diarization into the on-device recording flow in useRecorder: request embeddings on finals, assign live labels, persist vectors, refine at stop and push the result through sync, plus a live rename API for the recorder screen.

Purpose: this is where SPK-01 (live labels), SPK-02 (refined + saved), SPK-03 (rename during recording) and SPK-05 (never blocks transcription: everything is optional and lowest priority) come together.
Output: useRecorder.ts changes only.
</objective>
