## Phase Goal

**As a** person who records meetings on a phone and reads them on a laptop, **I want to** use an app that looks modern, reacts to sound and moves smoothly while staying simple, **so that** recording and sharing meetings feels alive and easy instead of static.

<objective>
Lay the Aurora Ink foundation so the whole app re-themes at once: design tokens (light + dark), Geist fonts, motion tokens and keyframes, glass utility, reduced-motion switches (OS + in-app attribute), new themeColor/manifest colours, and a never-throwing haptics helper.

Purpose: UI-01 (tokens/theme), UI-03 (motion tokens + reduced motion), UI-05 (contrast proven by test). User-visible slice: after this plan alone, every existing screen renders in the new palette and font with no component edits.
Output: globals.css, layout.tsx, manifest.ts, src/lib/haptics.ts, tests/contrast.test.ts.
</objective>
