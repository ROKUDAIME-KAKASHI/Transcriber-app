# Phase 8 Human UAT (Xiaomi phone, new APK)
Build and install the new APK first (`android/gradlew assembleDebug`; the native code changed). Use `chrome://inspect` over USB for the console notes. Pick Fast (Moonshine) unless a step says otherwise.

- [ ] 1. Capability gating: an old APK plus the new site records on-device exactly as before (web microphone, "keep the screen on"). The laptop website is unchanged. In the new APK the engine note under Fast/Accurate says it keeps recording with the screen locked.
- [ ] 2. Airplane mode, 1 h locked: new APK, airplane mode, Fast engine, tap record, lock the phone at once, play or hold a meeting for 60+ minutes, unlock. The "Recording" notification stayed visible. The transcript has no hole around the locked period (check the times of the parts).
- [ ] 3. Does the page keep running while locked? With `chrome://inspect` open, leave the screen off for 5 minutes and watch the page console and the part count. Note whether the transcript grew live or only after you unlocked.
- [ ] 4. Catch-up: if there was a backlog after unlock, the status reads "Catching up · N min of audio left (about M min)", the number goes down, and the live word preview is off while it does. Note how many minutes of catching up one minute of backlog needs. Parts made while catching up have speaker labels (not all one speaker).
- [ ] 5. No duplicates: search the transcript around the lock, unlock and resume moments for repeated sentences.
- [ ] 6. Notification Stop while locked and offline: tap Stop in the notification. A "Meeting stopped" notification appears. Open the app: it says it is transcribing what was recorded, then "Saved on this device". The meeting is DONE and the audio is gone (a second app start shows no recovery).
- [ ] 7. In-app Stop with a backlog: tap Stop while "Catching up" is showing. It says "Finishing transcription. N min of audio left", finishes, then "Saved on this device. It will upload when you're online." Turn the network on: it syncs.
- [ ] 8. Remote stop: with the phone online and unlocked, stop the meeting from the laptop Meetings list. The phone finishes the transcript. (While the phone is on another screen, or locked with the page frozen, the stop is noticed when you come back to Record.)
- [ ] 9. Kill recovery: swipe the app away mid-recording; the recording continues (notification stays). Reopen: it reconnects, with no duplicated or missing phrases, and the earlier part of the transcript is still there. Then Settings > Apps > Force stop during a recording and reopen: "Recording was stopped by Android at mm:ss. Transcribing what was recorded.", the meeting finishes, and it was never marked finished before that.
- [ ] 10. Pause and resume from the notification while locked: no phrase spans the pause, and the timer does not count the pause.
- [ ] 11. Battery and heat: note the battery drop per hour locked and whether the phone gets warm, for an hour in airplane mode.
- [ ] 12. Storage: with little free space (under about 1 h of recording) a "Phone storage" notice appears. Under 50 MB free the recording stops by itself, the notice says so, and what was recorded is still transcribed.
- [ ] 13. Leave and return: tap Meetings while recording (the pill shows and keeps timing), then back to Record. The recording carried on and the transcript catches up. Stop from the pill: it opens Record and finishes the transcript.
- [ ] 14. Meetings list: an unfinished offline recording shows "Needs finishing" (not "Not responding"), and Stop there finishes it on the phone instead of dropping the audio.
