# Simplify call recording on Record

Replaced the audio source selector, the "Recording an online call?" section, Join call, the CallGuide modal, the wrong-surface check, Share again and GuidedCall with one switch under the record button: "Also record computer sound" (desktop Chromium-style browsers only, hidden on phones and in the APK, remembered per device, on by default for a planned meeting with a call link). Pressing Record opens the share picker once. A tab, window or screen is fine if it carries audio; otherwise "No computer sound was shared..." and nothing starts. Stopping the share stops and saves. The silence warning stays (it is now a plain notice). The old "Also capture tab audio" checkbox and the `guidedCall` flag are gone. ScreenNamingToggle stays on Profile; screen naming runs when its opt-in is on and the switch is used (the platform comes from the plan's call link).

Echo: with the switch on, the mic opens with echoCancellation, noiseSuppression and autoGainControl on, and a GainNode on the mic ducks it to 0.1 (-20 dB) while the computer sound is above speech level (`src/lib/callDuck.ts`, pure and unit-tested; 300 ms hold, about 100 ms ramp up via setTargetAtTime). Trade-off: if you talk over a remote speaker, your words are quieter while ducked. Mic-only recording is unchanged.
